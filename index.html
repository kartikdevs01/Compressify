    NEED TO KNOW

    Frequently asked questions

    Everything you need to know before making your files lighter.

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?+

    No. Compressify uses your browser’s built-in image tools; your file stays on your device throughout.

    Which image formats work?+

    You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP files. Compressed downloads are delivered as efficient WebP images.

    Will compression reduce image quality?+

    Compression is lossy, but the quality slider lets you choose the best balance for your image. Higher values preserve more detail.
